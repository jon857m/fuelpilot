About FuelPilot

FuelPilot is a free UK fuel finder designed to help drivers quickly compare nearby petrol and diesel prices from over 8,000 fuel stations.

Using official UK Government Fuel Finder data, FuelPilot updates prices every 15 minutes and presents them on a clean, map-first interface without unnecessary clutter.

Whether you're planning a journey, looking for the cheapest local fuel or simply comparing nearby stations, FuelPilot aims to make finding fuel prices quick and straightforward.

FuelPilot is independently developed and maintained in the UK by Jon Margree. The service continues to evolve with new features, usability improvements and performance enhancements.
